# Category checks

Use the relevant checklist to decide what to verify and which comparables are actually comparable. These checks supplement, rather than replace, the listing-specific evidence.

## Electronics and gaming

Match the exact model, storage/capacity, carrier/region, revision, and included charger, controller, dock, or cables. Ask whether the device powers on, charges, connects, and resets normally. For phones/tablets, verify that activation and account locks can be cleared by the seller during an in-person handoff; never request credentials or instructions to defeat a lock. Watch for battery health, screen damage, stick drift, overheating, water damage, repaired parts, and copied serial stickers.

## Furniture and appliances

Measure dimensions, material, brand/model, age, and pickup constraints. Inspect for odors, smoke/pet exposure, pests, mold, structural damage, stains, missing hardware, and evidence of repairs. For appliances, test the core operating cycle when practical and account for delivery, installation, power requirements, and likely parts cost.

## Vehicles and powered equipment

Do not establish mechanical condition from photos. Match trim, mileage/hours, title status, service history, options, accident history, and regional market. Require a lawful title/ownership review, VIN history where appropriate, a test drive or operation test, and an independent inspection before treating the offer as sound. Treat salvaged, rebuilt, lien, registration, warning-light, and transport issues as valuation variables, not footnotes.

## Collectibles, luxury goods, and jewelry

Match edition, release, grade, provenance, completeness, and authentication status. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity. Compare only with genuinely equivalent grades and documented sold sales. For high-value items, advise a reputable third-party authentication or an authenticated platform before payment.

## Tools, bicycles, and outdoor equipment

Match model/revision, wear surfaces, battery/charger, safety components, maintenance, and consumable replacement cost. Check that serials have not been removed or altered, but do not publicly share full serial numbers. Recommend an operation test and a lawful proof-of-ownership check if the circumstances warrant it.

## Baby, medical, and safety-critical items

Check for recalls, expiration dates, damage, missing safety components, and whether a product is safe to transfer second-hand. Recommend against relying on an uncertain item where safety depends on its history, inspection, or proper installation.
